01INITIAL PILOT PRIORITIES

The essential
school workflows.

These are proposed starting modules for pilot discovery. The exact feature list and acceptance criteria will be agreed before implementation.

01
CORE FOUNDATION

School setup and academic structure

School profile, academic year, classes, sections, basic configuration and authorised user setup.

  • School and academic-year settings
  • Class and section structure
  • Role and permission planning
PILOT PLANNING
02
CORE FOUNDATION

Admissions and student records

Organise student identity and school records so authorised staff can find and maintain consistent information.

  • Student profile and admission details
  • Guardian and contact information
  • Class/section assignment and record updates
PILOT PLANNING
03
CORE FOUNDATION

Daily attendance

Support classroom attendance routines and give authorised users a clearer view of records.

  • Class-wise attendance entry
  • Attendance review and correction workflow
  • Basic attendance summaries
PILOT PLANNING
04
CORE FOUNDATION

Fee administration

Bring fee configuration and collection records into an organised, reviewable workflow.

  • Fee heads and schedules
  • Payment status and collection entries
  • Receipt references and outstanding views
PILOT PLANNING
05
CORE FOUNDATION

Notices and communication

Help staff prepare consistent school notices and record what has been shared with an intended audience.

  • Notice drafting and categorisation
  • Audience selection
  • Delivery integration assessment
PILOT PLANNING
