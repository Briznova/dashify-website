DASHIFY DESIGN PRINCIPLE

A connected view of school roles

These visuals describe the proposed experience and working approach. Final screens, permissions and available features will be confirmed as the product is built and validated with schools.

01ROLE-BASED WORKFLOWS

Different roles.
One organised system.

These are intended role journeys. Final permissions and workflows will be designed with each pilot school and reviewed before live use.

01 / LEADERSHIP
◎

Principal and school administrator

Oversee the school’s operating structure and review information relevant to day-to-day management.

  • Review school setup and class structure
  • Monitor attendance and fee summaries
  • Manage authorised users and responsibilities
  • Prepare and review school notices
02 / TEACHING
▤

Teachers and class coordinators

Complete recurring classroom tasks through a focused workflow that avoids unnecessary administration.

  • View assigned classes and student lists
  • Record daily attendance
  • Review permitted student information
  • Receive relevant school announcements
03 / ACCOUNTS
₹

Accounts and fee office

Keep fee configuration, collection entries and follow-up records organised and reviewable.

  • Maintain fee categories and schedules
  • Record collections and references
  • Review outstanding fee information
  • Prepare basic collection summaries
04 / FAMILIES
⌂

Parents and students

Parent and student self-service is a possible later phase, subject to school requirements and privacy controls.

  • Potential attendance visibility
  • Potential access to school notices
  • Potential fee information access
  • Potential academic updates
NOT PART OF INITIAL PILOT COMMITMENT
02ACCESS PRINCIPLES

Access should follow responsibility.

Useful visibility.
Appropriate boundaries.

A class teacher does not need the same access as an accounts administrator. A parent should not be able to browse another family’s information. Permission design must reflect the school’s real responsibilities.

DASHIFY’s planned access model includes role-based permissions, separation of school workspaces and reviewable administrative actions. These controls must be implemented and tested before live student information is introduced.
