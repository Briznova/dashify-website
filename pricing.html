01WHY NO PRICE TABLE YET

No surprise promises.

Price should follow
a clear scope.

School size, users, modules, data migration needs, support expectations and integrations can all affect the effort involved in an implementation.

Publishing a premature price would create the wrong expectation. We will validate workflows, define the pilot scope and confirm service responsibilities first. Any commercial proposal will be shared in writing for review.

02WHAT A FUTURE PROPOSAL MAY COVER

Understand what
you are paying for.

When commercial plans are ready, proposals should make service boundaries and support clear.

01

School and user scope

The school workspace, user roles, expected usage and any agreed multi-campus requirements.

02

Included modules

The exact modules, workflows and limits included in the agreed subscription or pilot.

03

Setup and migration

Any configuration, training, data preparation or migration activities, listed separately.

04

Support arrangements

Support channels, operating hours, response expectations and escalation process.

05

Optional integrations

Payment, messaging, biometric or third-party integrations and related provider charges.

06

Renewal and exit terms

Billing cycle, renewal, data export, retention and termination arrangements.
