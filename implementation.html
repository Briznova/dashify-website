DASHIFY DESIGN PRINCIPLE

A practical implementation journey

These visuals describe the proposed experience and working approach. Final screens, permissions and available features will be confirmed as the product is built and validated with schools.

01PROPOSED IMPLEMENTATION JOURNEY

Five considered
steps to a pilot.

The sequence is a proposed delivery approach. Timing will be agreed only after scope and readiness are understood.

01
DISCOVER

Understand the current process

Meet school stakeholders, map records and routines, identify pain points and agree what success should look like for a first phase.

  • Workflow discussion
  • Roles and responsibilities map
  • Prioritised requirements
02
DEFINE

Agree scope and responsibilities

Confirm what is in scope, who will use the system and what the school needs to prepare.

  • Written pilot scope and acceptance criteria
  • Named school coordinator
  • Data handling and access decisions
03
CONFIGURE

Set up the agreed foundation

Prepare the school structure and test workflows using synthetic or specifically approved sample data before considering live information.

  • Academic structure setup
  • Role and permission review
  • Controlled data preparation plan
04
ENABLE

Train the people doing the work

Walk administrators, teachers and accounts users through agreed responsibilities and collect questions before the pilot begins.

  • Role-based walkthroughs
  • Quick reference guidance
  • Support and escalation process
05
LEARN

Review and improve

Review how workflows perform, capture issues and decide whether to refine, extend or pause the pilot.

  • Issue and feedback log
  • Scope and outcome review
  • Next-phase recommendation
02WHAT THE SCHOOL PREPARES

A good pilot is
a shared effort.

Clear ownership and preparation help keep implementation focused and reduce disruption to the school day.

01

A school coordinator

One point of contact who can coordinate decisions, arrange user sessions and consolidate feedback.

02

Process knowledge

An understanding of current admission, attendance, fee and communication routines.

03

Approved sample data

Synthetic data for demonstrations, or an approved data set only after safeguards are ready.
